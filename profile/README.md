# Trap & Femboy Vietnam

**Trap & Femboy Vietnam** is a community-driven organization focused on supporting, connecting, and empowering feminine-presenting boys, femboys, gender-nonconforming people, and allies in Vietnam and the Vietnamese diaspora.

We are a **safe, active community** especially suitable for femboys and gender-nonconforming people who want to freely express themselves without judgment, shame, or pressure to follow traditional gender expectations.

---

## 🦈 Who We Are

We are a grassroots community built by and for:

- ✨ Femboys  
- 🎀 Gender-nonconforming individuals  
- 🏳️‍⚧️ Trans and questioning people  
- 🤝 Supportive friends & allies  

Our symbol — the shark — represents comfort, community, and soft strength. Like the ocean, we believe gender expression is wide, deep, and beautifully diverse.

---

## 🌸 Our Mission

Our goals are to:

- 💖 Maintain a **safe and welcoming space** for self-expression  
- 🌟 Build an **active community** where members can interact, share, and grow  
- 📢 Raise awareness about gender expression diversity in Vietnam  
- 🧠 Share resources about identity, confidence, and well-being  
- 🤝 Build friendships and mutual support networks  
- 🎨 Celebrate fashion, aesthetics, and creativity beyond gender norms  

We believe femininity is not limited by gender, and everyone deserves the freedom to explore who they are.

---

## 🫶 Our Community

At the heart of **Trap & Femboy Vietnam** is people.

We are a **safe, active, and supportive community** where members can:

- 🌸 Express their feminine side freely  
- 💬 Talk about daily life, interests, and hobbies  
- 🎀 Share outfits, fashion, and aesthetics  
- 🎮 Hang out, play games, and relax together  
- 🤝 Make real friendships with people who understand  

Many members join because they want a place where they don’t have to hide who they are. Here, you don’t need to act “more masculine” or follow social expectations — you’re welcome as you are.

### 💖 A Space to Be Yourself

This community is especially suitable for:

- Femboys who want to explore or express their style  
- People questioning their identity or presentation  
- Those who feel different and want understanding friends  
- Anyone who values kindness, respect, and open-mindedness  

You can be shy, loud, new, experienced, or still figuring things out — there is no “correct” way to be here.

### 🌟 Active & Friendly Environment

We aim to keep the community:

- **Active** — conversations, events, and shared activities  
- **Friendly** — no judgment for appearance or identity  
- **Supportive** — members help each other grow in confidence  
- **Comfortable** — a place to relax, not a place for pressure  

Whether you’re here to talk, share your style, code, game, or just quietly belong — you have a place with us.

## 🧷 What We Do

Our community is designed to be **comfortable, supportive, and engaging**, including:

- 💬 Community chat groups & discussion spaces  
- 📷 Fashion, makeup, and style sharing  
- 🎮 Gaming and social hangouts  
- 🎉 Online events & themed community days  
- 📚 Resource sharing (mental health, identity, self-confidence)  
- 🛡 Peer support for those exploring their identity  

This is a space where femboys and GNC people can **express themselves openly and safely**.
